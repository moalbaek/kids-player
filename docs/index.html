🎵 Kids Player

A small touchscreen that plays our Spotify playlists on the Sonos speakers, with the album cover for every song.

How it works

  • Playlists whose name starts with Kids: show up on the player.
  • Tap a song: the playlist starts on the chosen Sonos speaker from that song.
  • The player talks to Sonos directly on the home network.
